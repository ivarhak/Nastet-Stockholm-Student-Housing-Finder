Privacy & terms

Who runs this

Nästet is a personal, non-commercial project by Ivar Håkansson, a student in Stockholm. It is free, carries no advertising, and nothing on it is for sale. To get in touch, open an issue on GitHub.

What is collected about you: nothing, unless you use the Discord bot

There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking cookies. I do not record what you search for, which listings you look at, or that you visited at all. The one exception is the optional Discord bot, described below — and only if you start it yourself.

What is stored on your device

Everything you set up on this site is kept in your own browser and never sent anywhere: nastet-theme (light or dark), nastet-profile (the queue days, dates, budget and move-in window you entered, and a watch), nastet-marks (listings you saved, applied to or hid), nastet-compare (your compare list), nastet-seen (which listings were up on your last visit, for "since your last visit"), nastet-allin and nastet-iso (two display switches). "Forget this device" under Queues to join deletes all of them. A profile link or a watch in the address bar carries those numbers inside the link itself — anyone you send it to can read them, and nobody else. Calendar reminders are built in your browser and downloaded straight to you.

Who your browser talks to

Loading any web page reveals your IP address to whoever serves it. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and its map tiles come from CARTO; everything else — the typefaces and the map library included — is served from this site itself. Those two companies see your IP address in their own logs, under their own privacy policies. I get nothing back from them — no report, no counts, no identities.

The Discord bot (optional)

If you use /watch set, the bot keeps, under your Discord user id: the city, your queue days and the date you gave them, max rent, areas, whether you want every new listing, the id of your DM channel with the bot, and which listings it has already told you about. That is all — no name, no messages. It is stored by Cloudflare (Workers KV), which runs the bot, and Discord itself of course handles the messages under its own policy. /watch stop deletes it immediately; a watch nobody has touched for six months is deleted automatically.

Your rights

If you have not used the Discord bot, I hold no personal data about you and there is nothing to show, correct or erase. If you have, /watch show displays everything kept and /watch stop erases it — or ask, and I will do it for you. You can also complain to Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), the Swedish data protection authority.

Not official

Nästet is not affiliated with, endorsed by or run by SSSB, Bostadsförmedlingen, SGS Studentbostäder, AF Bostäder, KTH or any other landlord or university. It reads listings those organisations publish openly and draws them on a map.

Apply at the source

Everything here is a copy, taken at the time shown at the top of the page, and it can be stale, incomplete or simply wrong — a listing may be gone, a queue figure may have moved, a deadline may have passed. Always open the landlord's own page and apply there. Nothing on this site is a promise of a home, a queue position or a deadline, and it is not advice.

If you publish listings

If you are a landlord or a housing service and would rather your listings were not shown here, say so and they will be taken down — no argument, no lawyer needed. Same link as above.

    1 SSSB queue

    Covers: all SSSB areas (Lappkärrsberget, Jerum, Kungshamra, …)

    Register at sssb.se. You collect one queue day per day, and listings go to whoever applies with the most queue days — that's the number this dashboard sorts by.

    To actually sign a contract you need to be a member of an SSCO-affiliated student union (or if you aren't registered for classes, an admissions letter works as well) and meet their study-pace requirements — check their current rules on the site.

    Do this first: registering is quick and days only start counting from when you register, so every week earlier is a week higher in every future queue. You can collect a maximum of 90 days before registering with a student union (class registration required).

    2 Bostadsförmedlingen

    Covers: Svenska Bostäder student apartments + other landlords' student housing across Stockholm

    This is Stockholm's city housing agency — Svenska Bostäder doesn't run its own student queue, their listings are advertised here. Register at bostad.stockholm.se; there's a yearly queue fee, and offers go by queue time.

    Student listings here often have much shorter effective queues than regular apartments — and unlike SSSB there's no student-union requirement to register, though student listings require you to be studying when you sign.

    Note: ads here show an application deadline ("sista ansökningsdag") rather than a required queue-days figure — you apply and the longest-queued applicant wins, so it costs nothing to try even with few days.

    Strategy

    Join both queues ASAP, long before moving — they're independent and both reward early registration. Use SSSB queue days for the areas closest to most university areas (Lappkärrsberget, Jerum, Kungshamra are the classic first-year picks), and treat Bostadsförmedlingen as a good backup plan, as on average those offers are more expensive.
